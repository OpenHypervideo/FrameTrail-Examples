A Warming World, Year by Year
=============================

Map film
    "Global Temperature Anomalies from 1880 to 2025", NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, entry 5603:
    https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5603
    Visualizer: Mark SubbaRao. Scientists: Gavin A. Schmidt, Robert B. Schmunk, Reto A. Ruedy.
    Public domain. Used here: the HD "smoothed" version, slowed from 2.5 years per second to one year per second
    (frames blended). Nothing else was changed.

Figures
    NASA GISS Surface Temperature Analysis (GISTEMP v4), global annual means (January to December),
    compared with the 1951-1980 average, as downloaded in November 2026:
    https://data.giss.nasa.gov/gistemp/
    GISTEMP Team, 2026: GISS Surface Temperature Analysis (GISTEMP), version 4. NASA Goddard Institute for Space Studies.
