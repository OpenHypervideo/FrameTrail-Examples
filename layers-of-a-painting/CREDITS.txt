Layers of a Painting
====================

Painting
    Hieronymus Bosch, "The Adoration of the Magi" (ca. 1475), oil and gold on oak.
    The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York, John Stewart Kennedy Fund, 1913 (13.26).

Images (all three: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, CC0, donated to Wikimedia Commons)
