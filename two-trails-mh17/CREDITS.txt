Two Trails: The Route of the Buk
================================

Film
    Joint Investigation Team MH17 / Netherlands Public Prosecution Service (Openbaar Ministerie), "3. MH17 Animation regarding the transport route and the launch site", shown at the team's presentation of 28 September 2016. The film is not copied: it plays from the Prosecution Service's YouTube channel through YouTube's embedded player (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sf6gJ8NDhYA).
    Presentation: https://www.prosecutionservice.nl/topics/mh17-plane-crash/criminal-investigation-jit-mh17/jit-presentation-first-results-mh17-criminal-investigation-28-9-2016

Captions
    YouTube's automatic English captions of the film, set as sentences, with the names of places, institutions and vehicles corrected for this example. The intercepted telephone calls in the film are not captioned; three notes quote them from the transcripts the film shows.

Materials beside the film
    What the film names, shows or rests on, each shown from where it is published; nothing of it is copied here. Which item belongs to which moment was decided for this example, from the film's narration and from its picture (the labels on its maps, its legends, the calls). The pages of open-source researchers and reporters are the ones Bellingcat's overview cites for the same sighting.

    Videos, embedded from YouTube: four further films of the investigation team (Openbaar Ministerie): an intercepted call of 16 July 2014, the weapon, the forensic investigation, the detonation tests; Bellingcat's copy of the video of the launcher in Zuhres; the video of the launcher in Luhansk as Ukraine's Ministry of Internal Affairs published it; a recording of the Zello radio channel of Torez, Shakhtarsk and Snizhne of 17 July 2014 (torezinfo).
    Post on X: the photograph of the smoke trail as it was posted on the afternoon of 17 July 2014, shown as the post's own embed.
    Pages shown in a frame from their own sites: the District Court of The Hague's MH17 site, Correctiv, Mashable, the blog Ukraine at War.
    Pages shown as a card with a link: Bellingcat (ten articles and its report; the site announces that it will not allow framing), the Netherlands Public Prosecution Service, the Dutch police, the Dutch Safety Board, Paris Match, The Guardian, BuzzFeed News, The Daily Telegraph, RTL Nieuws.
