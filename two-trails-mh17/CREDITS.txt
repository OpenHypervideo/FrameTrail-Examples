Two Trails: The Route of the Buk
================================

Film
    Joint Investigation Team MH17 / Netherlands Public Prosecution Service (Openbaar Ministerie),
    "3. MH17 Animation regarding the transport route and the launch site", shown at the team's
    presentation of 28 September 2016. The film is not copied: it plays from the Prosecution Service's
    YouTube channel through YouTube's embedded player (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sf6gJ8NDhYA).
    Presentation: https://www.prosecutionservice.nl/topics/mh17-plane-crash/criminal-investigation-jit-mh17/jit-presentation-first-results-mh17-criminal-investigation-28-9-2016

Captions
    YouTube's automatic English captions of the film, set as sentences, with the names of places,
    institutions and vehicles corrected for this example. The intercepted telephone calls in the film
    are not captioned.
