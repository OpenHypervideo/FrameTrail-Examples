Into the Black Hole
===================

Films
    "NASA Black Hole Visualization Takes Viewers Beyond the Brink" (2024), NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio,
    entry 14576: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/14576
    Credit: NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center / J. Schnittman and B. Powell.
    Visualizer: Jeremy Schnittman. Producer: Scott Wiessinger. Science writer: Francis Reddy.
    Support: Brian Powell, Ernie Wright.
    Public domain. Used here: the plunge in Mollweide projection (scaled to 1920 x 960, 30 frames per second,
    the grey surround of the oval darkened), the "Times" film (cropped to its box) and the "Inset" film,
    both starting 7 seconds in. No music and no NASA explainer footage is used.

Explanations
    Written for this example, in English and German. Public domain (CC0). What they say follows the labels of
    NASA's own explainer film and its published captions; this example is not made or endorsed by NASA.
